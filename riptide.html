Riptide — LOX/LNG Lander

SEDS UCSD's in-house R&D effort in active engine control — throttling and thrust vectoring — built around Riptide, a bi-propellant LOX-LNG sounding vehicle designed for suborbital flights to 1,000 ft with a powered landing.

Structures & Manufacturing

  • Designed angled solenoid manifold mounts and manufactured them using a metal bender and mill.
  • Resized the rocket body module length and mounting-hole pattern, then manufactured the module on a bandsaw, mill, and drill press.
  • Manufactured the landing legs, ankle joint, and footpad — generating CAM toolpaths in Fusion 360 to CNC and lathe stock metal.

Team page: sedsucsd.org/lander (not always up to date).
