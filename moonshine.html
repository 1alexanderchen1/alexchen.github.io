Moonshine Engine Chamber

A 600 lbf, 275 psi stainless-steel film-cooled thrust chamber and nozzle for a LOX/IPA bipropellant engine — sized in NASA's CEA, tuned for injector stiffness, and machined start to finish in the SEDS UCSD shop.

Design

  • Owned the design of the thrust chamber and nozzle, sized for 600 lbf thrust at 275 psi chamber pressure.
  • Used MATLAB's combustion toolbox, interfacing with NASA's CEA, to size throat area and nozzle expansion ratio.
  • Ran many design iterations to land on a target injector stiffness — balancing pressure drop against throttling range, shown in the 1C throttle map below.

Manufacturing

  • Manufactured the flange, chamber, nozzle, and propellant manifold on manual and CNC lathes and mills.
  • Built CAM toolpaths in Fusion 360 to drive CNC manufacturing of the chamber and nozzle.
